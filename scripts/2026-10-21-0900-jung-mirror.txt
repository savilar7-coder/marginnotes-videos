They said, just ignore the people who annoy you.

But Carl Jung saw it differently. His idea, in plain words: the people who bother you most are showing you yourself.

If their arrogance bothers you... maybe it's the confidence you never let yourself have.

If their laziness bothers you... maybe it's the rest you never let yourself take.

If their neediness bothers you... maybe it's the help you're too proud to ask for.

If their loudness bothers you... maybe it's the voice you swallowed years ago.

Jung called it the shadow. The parts of you that you hid so well, you only see them in other people.

Which one is yours? Tell me in one word.

And send this to the person you thought of first.

Because the next time someone gets under your skin, you'll remember. They said...
