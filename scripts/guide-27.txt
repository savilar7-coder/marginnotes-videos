Lesson 27. When you keep going back to what hurts.

In Dostoevsky's words: "Man is sometimes extraordinarily, passionately, in love with suffering, and that is a fact."

Sometimes we return to pain because it is familiar, and familiar feels safe. Seeing that you do this is the first step to stopping.

Tonight: Name the thing you keep going back to. Ask what it gives you that feels like comfort.
