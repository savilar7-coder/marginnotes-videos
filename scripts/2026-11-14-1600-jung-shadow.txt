[briskly] The nicest person you know has a dark side. So do you.

Carl Jung put it simply: [slowly] "Everyone carries a shadow."

Anger. Envy. Wanting more. But hiding it does not kill it.

Jung met his own in December 1913. In a dream, he shot the hero he wanted to be. He woke up shaken.

[quietly] Meet your shadow on purpose, or it will meet you by surprise.
