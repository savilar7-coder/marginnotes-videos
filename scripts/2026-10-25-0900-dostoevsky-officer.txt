[briskly] He could forgive a punch. Not being ignored.

An officer took him by the shoulders, moved him aside, and walked on. [pause] As if he wasn't there.

In Notes from Underground, a poor clerk says:

[slowly] "I could have forgiven blows, but I could not forgive his having moved me without noticing me."

So for years, he followed the officer. Learned his name. Paid a porter to find out where he lived. He wrote a story to mock him. [pause] It was rejected.

Then, the plan. On Petersburg's main street, he would not step aside.

[faster] He borrowed money from his boss. Black gloves. A hat. A beaver collar, like an officer's.

Six inches away, his nerve broke. He fell at the man's feet. [pause] The officer stepped over him.

At last, eyes closed... shoulder to shoulder. The officer didn't even look round.

He went home singing Italian arias.

Years of war. [quietly] And only one man was fighting.

[whispers] Who's your officer?
