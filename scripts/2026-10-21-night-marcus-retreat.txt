You keep thinking peace is somewhere else.

A beach. A cabin. A new city. A life where nobody needs anything from you.

Marcus Aurelius thought that too. And he was the emperor of Rome. He could have gone anywhere.

But in his private notebook, he wrote this to himself:

"A man cannot any whither retire better than to his own soul."

In other words, the quietest place you will ever find is inside you.

Not after the vacation. Not when the noise stops. Now.

Close your eyes for one breath. Notice that the storm is out there, and you are in here.

That place is always open. Nobody can take it from you.

Go back to it as often as you need.

Save this for the night you can't switch off.
