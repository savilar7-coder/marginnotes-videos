Lesson 19. When your mind spirals.

Marcus Aurelius wrote: "Let not thy mind wander up and down, and heap together in her thoughts the many troubles and grievous calamities which thou art as subject unto as any other."

An anxious mind loves to gather every possible disaster into one pile and stare at it. Marcus told himself to take troubles one at a time, as they actually come.

Tonight: If your mind starts listing disasters, stop at the first one and ask: is this one here yet?
