Lesson 14. When you can't get out of bed.

Marcus Aurelius wrote: "In the morning when thou risest unwillingly, let this thought be present,—I am rising to the work of a human being."

Even the emperor of Rome did not want to get up. He did not wait to feel motivated. He reminded himself what he was for, and got up anyway.

Tonight: Tonight, write the one reason you will get up tomorrow. Put it next to your phone.
