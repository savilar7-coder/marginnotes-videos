The twelfth of December. You are at your desk, going over your fears.

And you let yourself drop.

The floor goes. You land, not far down, in something soft. A cave. Icy water, knee-deep. A red stone, glowing. Under it a river, and a dead youth drifting by, and a sun coming up out of the water. Then blood, and for a long time it will not stop.

You write it down. Within days, you go back.

And now things have shapes.
