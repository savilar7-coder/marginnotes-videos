Lesson 22. When you wear a mask all day.

Carl Jung's idea, in plain words: We all wear a mask for the world. The trouble starts when we forget we are wearing it.

A face for work, a face for family, a face for strangers. Masks are useful. But if you never take them off, you can lose track of who is underneath.

Tonight: Tonight, do one small thing only for yourself, with no one watching.
