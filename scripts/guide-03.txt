Lesson 3. When things didn't go your way.

Epictetus wrote: "Demand not that events should happen as you wish; but wish them to happen as they do happen, and you will go on well."

Fighting what already happened is the most tiring fight there is, because you can't win it. Accepting it is not giving up. It is the moment you get your energy back for the next move.
