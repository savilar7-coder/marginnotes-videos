Lesson 8. When worry runs ahead of you.

Seneca wrote: "some things torment us more than they ought; some torment us before they ought; and some torment us when they ought not to torment us at all."

Seneca gives worry three tricks: making things bigger, making them early, and making them up. Naming the trick takes away half its power.

Tonight: Pick the worry that is loudest tonight and say which trick it is: bigger, early or made up.
