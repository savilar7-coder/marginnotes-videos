Lesson 2. When you can't stop replaying it.

Epictetus wrote: "Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of things."

The event happened once. The story you tell about it happens every time you replay it. Epictetus is not saying it didn't matter. He is saying the second wound is the one you keep reopening.

Tonight: Say the event out loud in one plain sentence, with no adjectives. Notice how much smaller it sounds.
