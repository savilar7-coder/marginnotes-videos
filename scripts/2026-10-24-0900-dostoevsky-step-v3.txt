You're not lazy. You're scared.

Dostoevsky gave that thought to a man on his way to rehearse a murder. Wait till you hear what he's afraid of.

[slowly] "Taking a new step, uttering a new word is what they fear most."

So he does what we do. Plans. Talks. Waits.

[quietly] "It's because I chatter that I do nothing."

And what scares him? Not the murder. [pause] [whispers] His landlady. On the stairs.

Your step is just an email. A phone call. Take it today.

And next time you call yourself lazy...
