An official reads the death sentence. The prisoners take off their outer clothes and put on white execution shirts. A priest offers them a cross to kiss.

The first three men are led to posts and bound there. White hoods are drawn over their heads. Petrashevsky pushes his cap back and faces the rifles. The soldiers raise their weapons and prepare to aim.

Dostoevsky embraces the men beside him. Mikhail is elsewhere.

Then a drum beats the signal to stand down.

The rifles come down. The men at the posts are untied and brought back. An official reads another announcement: the Emperor has granted them their lives. Then he reads the sentences they will actually serve.
