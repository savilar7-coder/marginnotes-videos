They told you to fight your dark side. Jung said that's exactly why it wins.

The anger you swallow. The envy you would never admit. The part of you that just wants to be seen.

Push it down, and it doesn't leave. It goes underground.

Jung called it the shadow. And his warning was simple: whatever you refuse to look at inside you, you end up meeting outside, and you call it fate.

The same fight. The same kind of person. The same three a.m.

So he didn't say destroy it. He said go down first. Meet it. Name it.

And the next time it shows up, don't panic. Just say: ah. You again.

The strongest people you know didn't kill their shadow. They put it to work.

Because they told you to fight your dark side...
