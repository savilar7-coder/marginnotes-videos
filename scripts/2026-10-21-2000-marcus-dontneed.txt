You don't need a beach to find peace.

You don't need a cabin in the mountains.

You don't need a new city, a quieter life, or everyone to finally leave you alone.

Marcus Aurelius had all of that. He was the emperor of Rome.

And in his private notebook, he wrote this to himself:

"A man cannot any whither retire better than to his own soul."

The quietest place you will ever find is the one you carry.

Not after the vacation. Not when the noise stops. Now.

One breath. The storm is out there. You are in here.

Send this to someone who is tired of running.
