Why do the smartest people in the room go quiet?

Epictetus told his students: never proclaim yourself a philosopher. Don't lecture people about your principles. Show them by actions. At a dinner, don't explain how people ought to eat. Eat as you ought.

Then the line that stings. If anyone tells you that you know nothing, and it doesn't bother you, then you've really started.

Sheep don't throw up the grass to show the shepherd how much they ate. They turn it into wool and milk.

Enchiridion, chapter forty-six. What are you saying that you should be showing?
