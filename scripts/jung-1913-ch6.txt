Meanwhile you copy your visions, like a medieval scribe, into a huge red leather book, and paint them.

Slowly the pictures change. By 1918, every morning, you draw a small circle, a mandala: a picture of yourself that day. One bad morning it comes out broken. You come to believe it shows the whole of you, and its centre. You will name that too: the self.

In 1957, past eighty, you look back. Something burst out, you say, and flooded you, and came close to breaking you. And everything else, the shadow, the anima, the self, came out of it.

Forty-four years earlier, a letter said you might be ill, and could not see it.

You went down, and you looked.
