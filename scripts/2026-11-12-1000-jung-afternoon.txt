[briskly] At thirty-eight, Carl Jung lost the plan for his whole life.

In 1913 he broke with Freud. For years, he felt lost.

But that crisis became his life's work. Years later, he wrote: [slowly] "We cannot live the afternoon of life according to the programme of life's morning."

[quietly] Morning is for making a name. Afternoon is for finding out who you are without it.
