[briskly] In midlife, the plan that built your life starts to feel like a cage.

At thirty-eight, Carl Jung had it all: fame, a post in Zurich, Freud's chosen heir. Then it stopped working.

He wrote: [slowly] "We cannot live the afternoon of life according to the programme of life's morning."

The afternoon is not the end. Morning is for making a name. Afternoon is for finding out who you are without it.
