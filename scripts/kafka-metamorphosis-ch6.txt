Gregor has understood every word. He turns and crawls back to his room. At the door he looks back. His mother has fallen asleep.

The moment he is inside, the key turns. It is Greta, and she says: at last.

In the dark he finds he cannot move, and can hardly feel the apple. He thinks of his family with love. He must disappear: he believes it more firmly than his sister does. He lies there until a tower clock strikes three, and still sees the window begin to lighten. Then his head sinks to the floor.

In the morning the cleaning woman prods him with her broom and shouts that it is dead. His body is flat and dry. Now we can thank God, the father says.
