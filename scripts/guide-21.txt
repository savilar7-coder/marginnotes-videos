Lesson 21. When you feel you have a dark side.

Carl Jung's idea, in plain words: You don't heal by picturing only the light, but by getting to know your dark.

Everyone has a shadow: anger, envy, desire, the parts that were not welcome growing up. Pushing them down gives them power. Looking at them calmly takes it away.

Tonight: Write one feeling you are not proud of. Next to it, write what it was trying to protect.
