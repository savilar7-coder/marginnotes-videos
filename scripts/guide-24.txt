Lesson 24. When you're living someone else's dream.

Carl Jung's idea, in plain words: The heaviest thing a child can carry is the life their parents never lived.

Many of us chase goals that were never ours: a parent's wish, a family script. Jung thought the unlived life of the parents lands on the children.

Tonight: Ask: whose dream is this? If the answer isn't yours, you are allowed to put it down.
