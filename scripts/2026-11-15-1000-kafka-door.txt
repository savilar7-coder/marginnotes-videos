In Kafka's parable, a man waits at an open door for years. The guard says: not now.

He grows old there. Dying, he asks why no one else ever came. The guard shouts: [slowly] "This entrance was meant only for you. I am now going to shut it."

[quietly] The door was open the whole time. He only asked. He never walked in.
