[briskly] Still waiting for permission?

In Kafka's parable, a man waits at a door for years. The guard keeps saying: not now.

So he gives the guard everything he owns. The guard takes it all, [slowly] "so that you don't think you've left anything undone."

Dying, the man asks why no one else ever came. [slowly] "This entrance was meant only for you. I am now going to shut it."

[quietly] What door are you sitting outside?
