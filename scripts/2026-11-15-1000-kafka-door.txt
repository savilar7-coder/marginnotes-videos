[briskly] A man waited his whole life at a door built only for him.

In Kafka's parable, the guard keeps saying: not now.

So he gives the guard everything he owns. The guard takes it all, and still says: not now.

Dying, the man asks why no one else ever came. [slowly] "This entrance was meant only for you. I am now going to shut it."

[quietly] Still waiting for permission?
