[briskly] Still waiting for permission?

In Kafka's parable, a man comes to a door. A guard says: not now. So he sits down, and waits. For years.

He grows old. He even begs the fleas in the guard's fur collar.

Dying, he asks why no one else ever came. The guard shouts: [slowly] "This entrance was meant only for you. I am now going to shut it."

[quietly] What door are you sitting outside?
