[briskly] The easiest person to lie to is you.

In The Brothers Karamazov, a monk tells a shameless old liar: [slowly] "Above all, don't lie to yourself."

Because it spreads. First you stop seeing the truth. Then you stop respecting anyone. And the last step is the strangest: you take offense, because it feels good.

[quietly] What did you tell yourself this morning that you would never say out loud?
