[briskly] You've told yourself the same small lie so long, it sounds true now.

In The Brothers Karamazov, an old monk tells a shameless liar: [slowly] "Above all, don't lie to yourself."

Lie to yourself long enough, he says, and you can't tell the truth inside you anymore. Then you stop respecting anyone.

[quietly] Every lie you tell others starts as one you told yourself.
