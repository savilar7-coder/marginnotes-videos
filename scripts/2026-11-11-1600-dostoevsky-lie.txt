[briskly] The easiest person to lie to is you.

In The Brothers Karamazov, an old monk tells a shameless liar: [slowly] "Above all, don't lie to yourself."

He does not scold him. He warns him. A man who lies to himself stops seeing the truth. Then he stops respecting anyone. Then he takes offense, just because it feels good.

[quietly] What did you tell yourself this morning that you would never say out loud?
