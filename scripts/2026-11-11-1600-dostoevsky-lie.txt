[briskly] The easiest person to lie to is you.

In The Brothers Karamazov, an old monk tells a shameless liar: [slowly] "Above all, don't lie to yourself."

The liar kneels, jokes, plays the fool. The monk answers anyway.

A man who lies to himself, he warns, stops seeing the truth. Inside him. Around him.

[quietly] So what lie did you tell yourself today?
