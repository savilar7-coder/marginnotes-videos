Lesson 6. When someone insults you.

Epictetus wrote: "Remember that it is not he who gives abuse or blows, who affronts, but the view we take of these things as insulting."

Words only hurt when you agree with them somewhere inside. The insult is theirs; the meaning you give it is yours. You can refuse the package.

Tonight: Before you answer anyone tonight, wait ten seconds. Most replies die in those ten seconds.
