Lesson 25. When you feel lost in your 30s or 40s.

Carl Jung's idea, in plain words: The afternoon of life cannot run on the morning's plan.

What worked at 20 often stops working at 40. Jung saw this not as failure but as a turn: the second half of life asks different questions.

Tonight: Write the question your life is asking you now. It is probably not the one it asked at 20.
