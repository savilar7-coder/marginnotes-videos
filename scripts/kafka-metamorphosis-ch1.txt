Kafka's Metamorphosis has a happy last page.

The story opens on a man's body in a bed, and ends on a body that is not his.

The man has woken as a monstrous insect, and will never change back. He is Gregor Samsa, a travelling salesman, and what worries him is the clock. His train left at five. It is half past six. His parents owe his boss money, and only Gregor earns. His father, an old man in a dressing gown, has not worked in five years.

So he tries to get up.
