1952. Albert Camus's friend Sartre tears into him in public. Paris takes Sartre's side.

That winter, Camus goes back to Algeria. To Tipasa, the Roman ruins by the sea where he was young.

And there, in the cold, he finds in himself [slowly] "an invincible summer."

[quietly] Everyone turned on him. Winter could not take that.
