Lesson 20. When someone gets under your skin.

Carl Jung's idea, in plain words: The people who bother you most are often showing you a part of yourself you haven't met yet.

Jung noticed that we see in others what we refuse to see in ourselves. The irritation is a clue, not proof that they are terrible. It points at something you hid.

Tonight: Name the trait that annoys you most in someone. Ask honestly: where is that in me?
