Carl Jung never said this.

"Until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life, and you will call it fate."

It's on a million posts. It isn't in any of his books.

What he actually wrote, in Aion, is stranger. When something inside you is never made conscious, it doesn't go away. It happens to you from the outside, as fate.

The same fight. The same partner. The same boss, in every new job.

Jung's question isn't "why does this keep happening to me?" It's: what am I not seeing in me?

Follow for sourced philosophy. No fake quotes.
