[briskly] You got everything you wanted. So why does it feel empty?

1913. Carl Jung has fame and money. And he has just lost Freud, his mentor and friend.

Then, at the top, the visions start. A flood of blood over Europe. He fears he is going mad.

But he does not run. He writes to his own soul. Sixteen years of pages, to find what the fame had buried.

[quietly] So when it feels empty, do not run. Write to it.
