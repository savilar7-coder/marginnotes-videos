[briskly] Nowhere to turn. Have you been there?

In Crime and Punishment, a drunk clerk asks a stranger: [slowly] "Do you understand, sir, do you understand what it means when you have absolutely nowhere to turn?"

The stranger is Raskolnikov. In days, he will do something terrible. But tonight, he walks the drunk man home, and leaves his coins on the window.

[quietly] When someone has nowhere to turn, be the somewhere.
