[briskly] In Crime and Punishment, the kindest act comes from a killer.

A drunk clerk turns to a stranger: [slowly] "...what it means when you have absolutely nowhere to turn?"

The stranger is Raskolnikov. In days, he will kill a woman with an axe. But tonight, he walks the drunk man home, and leaves his coins on the window.

[quietly] When someone has nowhere to turn, be the somewhere.
