Someone talked about you behind your back?

Here's the best comeback in two thousand years.

Epictetus was born a slave, and became one of Rome's great teachers. In his handbook he says: if someone tells you that a certain person speaks ill of you, don't make excuses. Just answer: "He was ignorant of my other faults, else he would not have mentioned these alone."

No anger. No explaining. You agree, you smile, and the insult has nowhere to land.

Enchiridion, chapter thirty-three. Try it the next time someone brings you gossip.
