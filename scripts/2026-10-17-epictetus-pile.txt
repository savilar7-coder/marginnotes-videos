You're anxious about the wrong pile.

Epictetus was born a slave, almost two thousand years ago. His handbook opens with one sorting rule.

"There are things which are within our power, and there are things which are beyond our power."

Within your power: your opinions, what you want, what you do next.

Beyond it: your body, your reputation, what they think of you. Whether they text back.

Tonight, write every worry down. Sort it into two piles.

Pile two, you let go. Pile one gets one action, tomorrow.

Save this for tonight.
