[briskly] When did you last ask how you are? Not your job. You.

One November night in 1913, Carl Jung opened a notebook and wrote to his own soul: [slowly] "My soul, where are you?"

He'd been away for years. Busy with patients, books, his name.

[quietly] Not "what should I do?" but "where did I go?" That question started the strangest book he ever wrote.
