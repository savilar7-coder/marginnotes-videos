[briskly] In 1944, Carl Jung's heart nearly stopped.

He was sixty-eight. In his visions, he floated high above the Earth, ready to leave it all.

Then his doctor pulled him back. And Jung was angry to return.

But the years after were his most fruitful. He said he had learned to say yes to his own life.

[quietly] What would you say yes to, if you had almost left?
