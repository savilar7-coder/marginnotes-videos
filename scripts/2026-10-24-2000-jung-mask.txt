They told you to just be yourself. Jung asked: which one?

The one at work. The one with your family. The one you are at two a.m., alone with your phone.

Jung called the face we show the world the persona. A mask. And he didn't say rip it off. Everyone needs one.

The danger is when you forget it's a mask. When the role starts wearing you.

You laugh at jokes you don't find funny. You want things because someone said you should. You become your job title.

So ask one question about everything you do today: is this real, or performed?

Real gets lighter over time. Performed gets heavier.

Jung said growing up starts right there. Noticing the mask, and choosing what's underneath.

Because they told you to just be yourself...
