He didn't stop. He'd already earned his degree. Two small books and newspaper work. By 1940, Paris, and a job at a big newspaper.

Then the German army came in. The paper fled south, and he went with it, while the country fell apart around him.

In December he married. Around the same time, the paper let him go.

So in January 1941 he went back across the sea to Oran, in Algeria, where his wife's family lived. No steady job. Bad lungs. Living in someone else's house. And on the table, a manuscript he was trying to finish, about whether life is worth living at all.
