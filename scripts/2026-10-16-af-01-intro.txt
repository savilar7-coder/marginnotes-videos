Welcome to Amor Fati.

In 1882, on New Year's Day, Friedrich Nietzsche wrote a wish for himself. He wanted to learn to see what is necessary in things as beautiful. He gave it a name: amor fati. Love of fate.

It doesn't mean everything happens for a reason. It doesn't mean pretending the hard things were good. It means this: what happened is mine, and I won't spend my life wishing it hadn't.

Tonight, three questions. I'll ask each one, then give you quiet time to think, or to write in your journal.
