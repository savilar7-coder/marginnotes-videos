There's a secret you've never told anyone. And there's one you've never even told yourself.

Dostoevsky wrote about it, in Notes from Underground:

"Every man has reminiscences which he would not tell to everyone, but only to his friends."

"He has other matters in his mind which he would not reveal even to his friends, but only to himself, and that in secret."

"But there are other things which a man is afraid to tell even to himself."

Read that last line again.

The things you're afraid to tell yourself don't disappear. They run your moods. Your mornings. Who you text at two in the morning.

And here's the strange part. He said the more decent a person is, the more of these things they carry.

So if you feel like you're hiding something from yourself... you're not broken. You're just human.

Save this. And tonight, say one of them out loud. Only to yourself.

Because there's a secret you've never told anyone...
