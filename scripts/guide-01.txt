Lesson 1. When everything feels like too much.

Epictetus wrote: "There are things which are within our power, and there are things which are beyond our power."

Most of what weighs on you was never yours to carry: other people's moods, the past, the outcome. Your choices, your effort and your answers are yours. That is a smaller pile, and you can lift it.

Tonight: Write two lists on paper: mine and not mine. Tonight, only think about the first one.
