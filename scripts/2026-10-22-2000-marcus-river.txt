Whatever you're carrying tonight, it's already leaving.

Marcus Aurelius, emperor of Rome, wrote this in his private notebook:

"Time is like a river made up of the events which happen, and a violent stream; for as soon as a thing has been seen, it is carried away, and another comes in its place, and this will be carried away too."

The bad day. The message you're waiting for. The fear you went to bed with. All of it is already in the river.

But so is this moment. That's the other half.

Marcus wrote: "the present is the only thing of which a man can be deprived"

Everything is real. And everything is temporary. Both at once.

So don't spend tonight living in tomorrow. You can't lose a future you don't have yet.

Save this for the nights that feel permanent.

Because whatever you're carrying tonight...
