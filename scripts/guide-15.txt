Lesson 15. When you want peace and quiet.

Marcus Aurelius wrote: "nowhere either with more quiet or more freedom from trouble does a man retire than into his own soul"

You don't need a beach or a cabin to rest. The quietest place you will ever find is the one you carry with you, and it is open any time you close your eyes.

Tonight: Close your eyes for three slow breaths. Notice that the noise is out there and you are in here.
