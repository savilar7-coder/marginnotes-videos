[briskly] An emperor started every morning by expecting rude people.

Marcus Aurelius ran Rome. And before anyone spoke to him, he told himself this.

[slowly] "Begin the morning by saying to thyself, I shall meet with the busybody, the ungrateful, arrogant, deceitful, envious, unsocial."

Then the twist. [pause] He refused to let any of them make him angry.

[slowly] "Nor can I be angry with my kinsman, nor hate him."

[faster] The coworker who takes credit. The driver who cuts you off. The friend who never texts back.

Expected, they lose their power over you.

[quietly] So tomorrow, before your first coffee, expect them... and decide they won't decide your day.
