Carl Jung saw it in his patients. The pain they ran from came back as symptoms: [slowly] "Neurosis is always a substitute for legitimate suffering."

Here is the trap. The small pains feel safer than the big one. So they multiply. Short temper. Tight chest. Three a.m.
