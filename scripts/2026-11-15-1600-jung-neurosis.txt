[briskly] You have avoided one conversation for weeks. Now you can't sleep.

In 1937, at Yale, Carl Jung put it in one line: [slowly] "Neurosis is always a substitute for legitimate suffering."

The pain you dodge does not leave. It splits into small ones. Short temper. Tight chest. Three a.m.

[quietly] One honest hurt costs less than a thousand small ones. Have the conversation. Then sleep.
