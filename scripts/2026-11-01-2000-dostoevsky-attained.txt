[briskly] You finally get it. And it feels like nothing.

Dostoevsky gave that feeling to his Underground Man, a bitter clerk who watches everyone, including himself.

[slowly] "He loves the process of attaining, but does not quite like to have attained."

[faster] The chase. The promotion. The new phone. [pause] The moment you hold it, the fun stops.

[slowly] "Man is a comical creature."

Maybe the point was never the prize. It was the climb.

[quietly] So enjoy the getting there... before you finally get it.
