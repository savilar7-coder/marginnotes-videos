Lesson 9. When you feel you have no time.

Seneca wrote: "Nothing, Lucilius, is ours, except time."

You can earn money back and fix most mistakes. The hour you spend scrolling, resenting or waiting is gone for good. Seneca was not telling his friend to rush. He was telling him to choose.

Tonight: Look at how you spent today. Keep one hour tomorrow for something you chose on purpose.
