Lesson 12. When it hurts.

Seneca wrote: "Pain is slight if opinion has added nothing to it"

Seneca wrote this while he was ill. Pain is real, but the panic, the self-pity and the what-ifs you pile on top of it are extra weight. That part you can set down.

Tonight: Describe the pain only as it is, right now, without the story about what it means.
