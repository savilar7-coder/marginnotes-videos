Lesson 4. When you lost something.

Epictetus wrote: "Never say of anything, “I have lost it,” but, “I have restored it.”"

Nothing was ever fully yours: not people, not things, not time. You had them on loan, and that is exactly why they were precious. Grief is the price of having had something good.

Tonight: Write down one thing you are grateful you had, not sad you lost.
