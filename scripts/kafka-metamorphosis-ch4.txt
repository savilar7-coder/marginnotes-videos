Gregor runs out to help, and is still out when the father comes home. The old man in the dressing gown now stands straight, in a blue bank uniform with gold buttons. Is this still my father, Gregor thinks.

The father comes for him. Something small and red rolls past. An apple. Then another: the father has filled his pockets from the fruit bowl, and throws apple after apple. One skids off Gregor's back. The next sinks into it.

Nobody dares take it out. It rots in his back, and after that he can barely crawl, and hardly eats. Before work Greta pushes some food in with her foot; at night she sweeps it out, mostly untouched.
