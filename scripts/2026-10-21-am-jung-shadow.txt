The person who annoys you the most is showing you something about yourself.

That's not a nice idea. But Carl Jung built a whole life's work on it.

He called it the shadow. The parts of you that you hide, deny, and push away.

And here's the trick. What you refuse to face inside, you start seeing everywhere outside.

The coworker who always needs attention. The friend who never listens. The ex who was so selfish.

Jung's idea, in plain words: what bothers you most in other people is often the part of you that you haven't met yet.

That's shadow work. And it starts with one honest question.

Next time someone gets under your skin, don't ask, what's wrong with them?

Ask, what is this showing me about me?

It's uncomfortable. That's how you know it's working.

Save this for the next person who annoys you.
