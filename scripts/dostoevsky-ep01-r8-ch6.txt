The phone is still beside the plate. You pick it up. Your brother answers.

He is halfway through a story about his day when he forgets someone's name. He stops. Starts again. You glance toward the sink.

You wanted to be here. You also want him to get to the point.

The impatience has survived everything you just understood. You haven't become someone else. You are giving him some of the evening you wanted for yourself, and part of you is keeping track.

Is that attention, or another way of counting the minutes?

You turn the phone's screen away and listen. He remembers the name. The story continues. There is no way to know in advance which sentence you will remember years from now.

Tomorrow, the same excuse may return. You may use it. Tonight, you stay through the part of the story that takes too long.

When the call ends, the plate is still there. The cup is still upside down. Nothing about the room says that you have changed.

Could you value this evening without making it the beginning of a perfect life?
