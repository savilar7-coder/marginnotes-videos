Welcome to the Shadow Review.

The psychiatrist Carl Jung used the word shadow for the parts of ourselves we don't want to see. The traits we hide, deny, or push onto other people.

His idea, in our own words: what we never look at doesn't go away. It shows up anyway, in our reactions, our arguments, our patterns. Looking at it is how it loses its grip.

Tonight, three questions. I'll ask each one, then give you quiet time to think, or to write in your journal. Be curious, not harsh.

This is a reflection practice, not therapy. If anything feels heavy, stop, and talk to someone you trust.
