His mother knocks. Then his father. Then the chief clerk arrives from the office. Gregor apologises through the door. That was an animal's voice, the clerk says.

Gregor turns the key with his jaws, and the door opens.

The clerk runs for the stairs. The father, hissing like something wild, drives his son back into the bedroom with a walking stick and a newspaper. The door slams.

Behind that door, for now, he is still Gregor, and it is still his room.
