They say you're lazy. Dostoevsky said you're scared.

In Crime and Punishment, a young man lying in his tiny room says it out loud: "Taking a new step, uttering a new word is what they fear most."

Not failure. The first step.

So you talk about it. You plan it. You wait.

"It's because I chatter that I do nothing."

Take one step today.

Because they say you're lazy...
