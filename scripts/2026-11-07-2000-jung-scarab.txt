Carl Jung had a patient who trusted only logic.

While she told him a dream about a golden scarab, something tapped on the window behind him.

A beetle. Gold-green, like a scarab.

He caught it, and handed it to her. [slowly] "Here is your scarab."

He called it synchronicity.

[quietly] It proves nothing about the universe. But it got through to her.
