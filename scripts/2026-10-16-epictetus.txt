You're not anxious about everything. You're anxious about the wrong pile.

Epictetus was born a slave, almost two thousand years ago. He taught one sorting rule.

"Of things, some are in our power, and others are not."

In your power: what you think. What you do next.

Not in your power: what they think of you. Whether they text back. Yesterday.

Tonight, write every worry down, and put each one in a pile.

Pile two, you let go. Pile one gets one action, tomorrow.

Follow for sourced philosophy.
