You keep saying you'll start on Monday.

Epictetus heard that excuse two thousand years ago. And he answered it.

"How long, then, will you delay to demand of yourself the noblest improvements"

"You are no longer a boy but a grown man."

Think about it. Your first years were shaped by things you never chose. Your parents. Your school. Your fears.

That part wasn't your fault. But what you do with the rest of it is yours.

And Epictetus warned what happens if you keep waiting for the right day. He said you will

"insensibly continue to accomplish nothing"

Not in one dramatic failure. Slowly. One Monday at a time.

So he left one rule:

"remember that now is the combat, now the Olympiad comes on, nor can it be put off"

Not next week. Not when you feel ready. Now.

Send this to the friend who's been waiting for Monday.

Because you keep saying you'll start on Monday...
