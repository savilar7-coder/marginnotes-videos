The rock comes from an old Greek myth.

Sisyphus was a king, too clever for his own good. He tricked the gods, he even tricked death. So when the gods finally caught him, they chose the worst punishment they could think of: pointless work.

He has to push a huge boulder to the top of a mountain. Every time he gets it there, it rolls back down. He walks down after it, and starts again. Forever.

Camus admits the gods had a point. There's nothing more dreadful than work that's hard and leads nowhere.
