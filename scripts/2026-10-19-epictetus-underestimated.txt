Let them underestimate you.

Epictetus said it almost two thousand years ago: if you would improve, be content to be thought foolish and dull with regard to externals. Don't need people to think you know everything. And if they do think you're somebody, distrust yourself.

His reason is simple. You can't polish your character and your image at the same time. While you're absorbed in one, you'll neglect the other.

So stop correcting what they think of you. Use the time.

Enchiridion, chapter thirteen.
