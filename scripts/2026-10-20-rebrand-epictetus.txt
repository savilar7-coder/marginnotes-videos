You're not upset because of what happened.

You're upset because of the story you told yourself about it.

The text you read three times. The look they gave you. The silence.

None of it hurt you. Your view of it did.

Almost two thousand years ago, a man who was born a slave figured this out. His name was Epictetus. He wrote:

"Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of things."

And when someone provokes you, he said, "be assured that it is your own opinion which provokes you."

Think about that. The person who hurt you has no power over you, except the power you hand them.

So tonight, before you replay it again, ask one question. Is this the thing... or is it my view of the thing?

Because the thing is already gone. The view is yours to change.

Save this for the next time it gets loud.
