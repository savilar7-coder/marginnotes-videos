One day you'll realize the thing you feared never came.

One day you'll realize you rehearsed that conversation for nothing.

One day you'll realize the worst night of your life was only a night.

One day you'll realize you suffered for things that never happened.

Seneca realized it two thousand years ago. He wrote to a friend:

"We suffer more often in imagination than in reality."

And he named the three tricks fear plays on you:

"some things torment us more than they ought; some torment us before they ought; and some torment us when they ought not to torment us at all."

More than they should. Before they should. When they shouldn't at all.

Save this for the night your mind won't stop.

Because one day...
