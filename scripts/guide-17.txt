Lesson 17. When life feels unbearable.

Marcus Aurelius wrote: "neither that which is future, nor that which is past can hurt thee; but that only which is present."

You are not carrying one day. You are carrying every bad day behind you and every one you fear ahead. Put them down. You only ever have to get through this one.

Tonight: Ask: what is actually hard in this exact minute? Usually the answer is smaller than you think.
