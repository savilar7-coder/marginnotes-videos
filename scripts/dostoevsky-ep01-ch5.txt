The date is still December 22. Dostoevsky writes about the time he has lost: mistakes, idleness, the hours he let go by. Then, on the same page, he writes about the life ahead.

“Life is a gift, life is happiness, each minute might have been an age of happiness. … I shall be reborn to a better thing.”

He puts the promise in writing. He asks his brother to write back, to send news of the family. He says goodbye, then continues the letter. There are still instructions to give, still people to remember. He asks about the children. The letter that began with the firing squad carries messages for a household.
