Then, October. You are on a train, and everything outside the window goes under water. Yellow waves, from the North Sea to the Alps. Wreckage. Bodies, in thousands. Then the whole sea turns to blood. It lasts about an hour, and you are the only one who sees it.

Two weeks later it comes back. A voice inside you says: look at it. It is real.

For nine years you were a doctor in a Zurich asylum, among people who saw things like this. You know the word for it.

You also know that to understand the pictures you would have to go down into them. You are afraid you would lose yourself.
