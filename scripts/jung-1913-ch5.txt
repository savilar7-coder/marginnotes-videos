The next summer the war comes, and Europe does go under. The fear lets go of you. The flood, you decide, was never yours alone, and neither was the word you were afraid of: psychosis.

You keep going down, with a new question. If this is not madness, what is it? And how much of it is in everyone?

One Sunday the doorbell rings and rings, and no one is there. The house feels packed to the door with the dead. They have been to Jerusalem, they say, and did not find what they were looking for. You pick up a pen, and the house goes quiet. Three evenings, seven sermons to the dead.
