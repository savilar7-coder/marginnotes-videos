In a dream, you and a dark figure shoot a hero dead, and the dark one takes the lead. You wake, and a voice inside you says you must understand this dream at once. By your own account it leaves you no other way out, and there is a loaded revolver in the drawer by your bed. You lie there until you understand. The hero is you: your will to have your own way. It has to die. Years later you will name the dark one beside you: the shadow.

As you write, a woman's voice speaks inside you. What you are doing, she says, is art. You argue with her. You will name her too: the anima.

An old man comes, with a blind girl and a black snake. He says he is the prophet Elijah. In the asylum, you would have written that on a chart.
