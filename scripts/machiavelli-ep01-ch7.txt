But handwritten copies had been passing from hand to hand. Five years after he died, a printer in Rome set it in type. The Pope who allowed it was another Medici. Princes already knew these things. Now their subjects could read them too.

And it said what it said. Feared, not loved. Know how to do wrong. To be imitated by others.

An English cardinal, whose king had just had his brother beheaded, read it, and wrote that it was written by the finger of Satan.

Then, in Paris, French Protestants were massacred in their thousands. A lawyer wrote a book to explain it. He blamed the king's mother. And he blamed her teacher: Machiavelli.

The king's mother was a Medici. Her father was the Medici the book is dedicated to.

The family that never answered him would be remembered as his pupils.

After that, the name did not need the book. The Church banned it. The stage made it a villain. And psychologists today name three dark traits a person can carry. One of the three is his name.
